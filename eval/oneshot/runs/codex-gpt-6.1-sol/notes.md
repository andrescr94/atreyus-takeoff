Takeoff covers only the ELECTRICAL PLAN on each of the four sheets in plans.pdf. I visually checked each plan and its legend, extracted symbol centres from the PDF vector geometry, and removed coincident duplicate drawing paths. Coordinates are unscaled PDF points from the page's top-left. Counts are calculated directly from the device records; empty CL entries indicate no devices in the labelled closets.

Legend mapping: 3D = switch_3way_dimmer, D = switch_dimmer, F = switch_fan, VS = switch_vacancy, A = switch_sensor; SD/MA = smoke_co. FC dedicated outlets are receptacle. Half-filled duplex symbols are receptacle_half_hot. A 240 V outlet also marked GFI/WP is counted once as receptacle_240v. The B32 electrical panel is other; HVAC equipment outlines and plumbing symbols are background, not additional electrical devices.

Qualifications: the ceiling-fan symbols denote OPTIONAL ceiling fans / prewire only; each is counted once as ceiling_fan, without a second junction_box. The circular attic lights and their switches are assigned to the underlying LIVING, HALLWAY or HALL room. No plan fixture carries a VP designation, so bathroom downlights remain light_recessed rather than inferred vapor-proof fixtures.

Room uncertainty: open KITCHEN / DINING and LIVING areas have no drawn partition; assignments follow the labelled functional zones and countertop runs. On A2-111, KITCHEN and DINING are separate, with the upper floor-light row assigned to KITCHEN / DINING and the lower row to LIVING. Water-heater niches without a separate label are grouped with BATH / BATH 1 on A1/A3 and CL on A2/A4. The walk-in-closet light switch on A4 is physically outside the closet and is assigned to BED 1. Whole-home device totals are unaffected by these room-boundary interpretations.
