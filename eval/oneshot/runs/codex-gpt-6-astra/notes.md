- Used only `plans.pdf` as the source. Read each sheet's electrical legend/notes, isolated its ELECTRICAL PLAN, extracted vector symbol centres with PyMuPDF, and visually checked the plotted detections against enlarged plan renders. Coordinates are original PDF points, top-left origin; repeated drawing strokes were counted once.
- Counts are generated directly from the device list. Kitchen and Dining are separate on A2; the combined printed label `KITCHEN / DINING` is retained on A1 and A3. Repeated `CL` labels are aggregated. Attic devices are grouped under the printed `IN ATTIC` label; switches are assigned to their physical room, not necessarily the room they control.
- Followed annotations: D = dimmer, 3D = three-way dimmer, F = fan control, VS = vacancy, A = sensor. A receptacle marked 240V plus GFI/WP is counted once as `receptacle_240v`; FC outlets without AFI/GFI markings use `receptacle`. No VP rating or hood receptacle was inferred from location alone.
- The legend describes ceiling fans as optional/prewire: each drawn fan assembly is counted once as `ceiling_fan`, without separately counting its central J. The attic's plain circular light symbol is interpreted as `light_pendant`. `other` is the B32 electrical panel, one exterior panel per sheet. Plumbing, appliance outlines, structural posts, wiring arcs, and symbols in other drawings/legends are excluded.
- Main uncertainty is room attribution in open spaces: boundaries between Living and Kitchen/Dining are not drawn, especially A1/A2/A4. Counter/appliance devices stay with the kitchen zone; adjacent open-area devices follow the printed room labels. Unlabelled water-heater compartments are attributed to BATH (A1), CL (A2), and BATH 1 (A3/A4). These choices affect room subtotals, not home totals.
- Final device totals, including detectors and panels: A1-111 = 56; A2-111 = 67; A3-111 = 83; A4-111 = 98. JSON types, coordinates, duplicate locations, and room/home count reconciliation were checked.
