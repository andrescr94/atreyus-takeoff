## Does the match radius change the result?

Recall on the vector PDF when a detection must be within R points of the device (one-to-one, same type). 22 pt is used above; 1 pt = 1/72 in on paper, at 1/4 in = 1 ft that is 0.67 in of building, so 22 pt is about 1.2 ft. A third of the devices have a neighbour closer than 22 pt, which is why matching is one-to-one and must agree on type.

Ideas:

- **vision-page** (an image): brute force: the whole sheet as one image, Claude finds and types every device.
- **text** (text layer or OCR): read the device labels printed beside the symbols (GFI, AFI, 240V, 3D, SD...) and report each label as a device; unlabelled devices are missed by design.
- **template** (vector PDF (its legend)): crop each legend symbol and stamp-search the plan image for it at four rotations, mirrored too.
- **geometry** (vector PDF): group the drawing's stroke segments into symbols and match each to the sheet's legend.
- **vision-crop** (an image): Claude sees the legend crop and a tight crop of the electrical plan, with typing rules.
- **vision-consensus** (an image): vision-crop asked twice; agreement is the confidence, disagreements go to the top of the review queue.
- **agent** (an image (works on vector or scan)): a coding agent (Claude Code) works the sheet: zooms, reads symbols against the legend, draws its marks back and re-checks; schema-checked answer.
- **agent-pair** (an image (works on vector or scan)): the Claude agent and a Codex agent on the same sheet; agreement is the confidence, disagreements go first in review.
